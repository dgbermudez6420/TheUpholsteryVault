Frequently asked questions

Answers about the materials and how to use them
before you choose your collection.

What is included in the complete bundle?

For the current checkout price, you receive 120 upholstery projects and all five bonuses: fabric and foam, preproduction, customer measurements and orders, finishing, and tools. Everything is included in one bundle.

How will I receive the materials?

This is a digital PDF collection, not a physical book. Follow the access instructions provided after payment is confirmed.

Can I customize the pieces?

Yes. You can study the designs and adapt them to your customer’s measurements, fabrics, and needs. Always check the actual base and make test samples before production.

Are these tested plans or photos of completed pieces?

No. These are proposed designs with explanatory illustrations and nominal dimensions. You must validate measurements, cuts, and materials in your workshop. They are not photographs of tested furniture.

Does it include building every frame?

No. The focus is upholstery. Some pieces require suitable existing or prefabricated bases. The content does not replace structural design or certify load capacities.

Do I need experience or special tools?

The designs cover different skill levels. Start with simpler ones and use appropriate tools according to their safety instructions. Complex designs require experience. Results and income are not guaranteed.
